German Precision Measurement Technology

Rheintacho is a leading German manufacturer specializing in rotational speed measurement and monitoring solutions. With decades of engineering experience, the company delivers high-precision instruments trusted by industries worldwide.

Advanced Speed Measurement

Reliable Monitoring for Industrial Systems

Rheintacho develops advanced tachometers, speed sensors, and stroboscopes designed for accurate rotational speed measurement and machine diagnostics. These solutions help improve reliability, efficiency, and predictive maintenance in modern industrial environments.

Industrial Applications

Trusted by Global Industries

Rheintacho solutions are widely used in industries such as power generation, oil & gas, manufacturing, transportation, and machine building. Their products provide accurate speed monitoring and condition diagnostics for rotating equipment.
